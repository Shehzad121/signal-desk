📡 Radar - kaun si coin signal ke kitni qareeb hai

⚠️ Ye trade nahi hai. Radar sirf dikhata hai ke market ka mizaj kya hai aur signal kab ban sakta hai. Trade tab lo jab upar "signal active hai" ka card aaye ya Telegram par NAYA SIGNAL ka message aaye. Us card mein entry, stop-loss aur target sab likha hota hai.
Price live Binance se (page khula ho to har 15 second). "Andaza" wale numbers sirf aise hain ke agar abhi signal bana to stop/target lagbhag kahan hote; asli numbers signal ke waqt candle band hone par pakke hote hain.
Ye kaise kaam karta hai? (30 second)
  1. Bot teen timeframes (1h, 2h, 4h) par alag alag market check karta hai, har ghante. Upar dropdown se chuno kaunsa timeframe dekhna hai. Har timeframe ke apne signals aur apna record hai.
  2. Agar price pichle ~1 hafte ki sab se oonchi (ya neeche) jagah tod de aur bara trend usi taraf ho, to signal aata hai (1h/2h par sirf BUY).
  3. Signal par tum entry lete ho aur stop-loss + take-profit dono usi waqt exchange par laga dete ho. Phir kuch nahi karna: price jahan pehle lage wahan position khud band.
  4. Agar 3 din tak dono na lagen to deadline par khud band kar do.
  5. R = jitna risk liya. Stop lage to -1R, target lage to +4R. Sirf 35-40% trades jeetti hain, lekin jeet haar se 4 guna bari hoti hai, isi se edge banta hai.
